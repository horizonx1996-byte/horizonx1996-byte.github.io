Privacy Policy

MAGIC Jarvis · Last updated: November 8, 2026

MAGIC Jarvis is a private desktop assistant used only by the owner of the Wahnsinnswelt channels to publish their own videos and to view statistics of their own accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar), profile statistics (followers, likes, video count), the list of the owner's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, and the permission to upload and publish videos to the owner's own account.
  • YouTube, Instagram, Facebook Page and Telegram: the same kind of data for the owner's own accounts — profile statistics, own videos and their statistics, and publishing own videos.

How the data is used

Data is used only to show the owner their own statistics in the assistant and to publish videos the owner has prepared. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner's computer. The app has no server and no database outside that computer. Data is sent only to the official APIs of the respective platforms.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in the platform settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services). Revoking access and deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored data.
