MAGIC Jarvis

Private desktop assistant of the Wahnsinnswelt channels — „Das Verrückteste der Welt – in einer Minute erklärt.“

What it is. MAGIC Jarvis is a personal Windows desktop assistant used only by the owner of the Wahnsinnswelt accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What it does.

  • Shows statistics of the owner's own accounts (followers, likes, views of own videos) on TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Telegram.
  • Publishes the owner's own finished short videos with captions at a scheduled time.
  • Helps plan new videos based on what performed well.

Data. All tokens and statistics stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
